I designed a 3U CubeSat frame for the LEARNSat-1 mission with a focus on structural integrity, manufacturability, and compliance with CubeSat standards. I modeled the structure in Autodesk Inventor, ran it through thermal, modal, and static FEA to validate it, and put together the assembly procedures and bill of materials. UGA AIAA · Detachable Autonomous Flight

Autonomous Deployable Glider, 2025 DBF Glider Team Lead

Designed a detachable, autonomous glider with Arduino flight control and 3D printed parts.

Read More ▼

I designed and built a detachable, autonomous glider for the DBF competition. I optimized the aerodynamic profile in XFLR5, integrated Arduino and ArduPilot flight control with GPS and gyroscopic sensing, and fabricated the nosecone and deployment mechanism using 3D printing and composites. We went through several failed drop tests before reworking the deployment system enough to get a clean drop and fly. This project was actually my introduction to DBF, and it's the one that set me on the path toward aerospace.

UGA Motorsports · Vehicle Dynamics & Manufacturing

Formula SAE EV Suspension Design, Suspension Lead

Redesigned the suspension geometry, cutting camber gain by over 80% and improving cornering grip by 15%.

Read More ▼

For UGA Motorsports' electric Formula SAE car, I redesigned the suspension geometry using SolidWorks, OptimumKinematics, and FEA. This cut unnecessary camber gain by more than 80% and, through new tie rod designs, improved cornering grip by 15%. I also machined the uprights and rockers myself, using FEA to guide where we could remove material to save weight while still keeping a safety factor above 1.5.
